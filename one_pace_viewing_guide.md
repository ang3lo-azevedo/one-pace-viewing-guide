# One Pace Viewing Guide

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://onepace.net/_next/static/media/logo.06c_g4ssqmy3k.svg" alt="One Pace Logo" width="320"/>
</p>

One Pace is a fan edit of the One Piece anime that removes filler, repeated flashbacks, and pacing drag to keep the story closer to the manga while preserving canon content.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Curated Viewing Guide**  
> This is a comprehensive One Pace viewing guide combining the canon One Pace base with community-recommended One Piece Movies, OVAs, specials, and filler scenes that are widely agreed to be worth watching.

> [!NOTE]
> **Streaming Options (One Pace Default + Stremio Option):**
> - **Default Stream:** Episode title links point directly to the official [One Pace Web](https://onepace.net/en/watch) player and corresponding arc chapters.
> - **Stremio Option:** Direct `[[Stremio]]` links are provided alongside each episode for users streaming through the [Stremio Web](https://web.stremio.com) app and addon.
> - **Canonical Guides:** Arc chapter mappings align with the official [One Pace edits spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1HQRMJgu_zArp-sLnvFMDzOyjdsht87eFLECxMK858lA/edit?gid=0#gid=0).

> [!TIP]
> **SeriesGraph Color-Coded Ratings & Spoiler-Free Hover Tooltips:**
> - **SeriesGraph Ratings:** Episode and Arc ratings reflect weighted IMDb and community ratings fetched from [SeriesGraph - One Piece](https://seriesgraph.com/show/37854-one-piece), averaging scores across adapted anime chapters.
> - **Rating Color Tiers:**
>   - $\color{#1DA1F2}\text{(★ 9.7+)}$: Masterpiece / Pinnacle episode (Diamond Blue)
>   - $\color{#186A3B}\text{(★ 9.0–9.6)}$: Exceptional (Emerald Green)
>   - $\color{#28B463}\text{(★ 8.0–8.9)}$: Great (Vibrant Green)
>   - $\color{#F4D03F}\text{(★ 7.0–7.9)}$: Good (Gold / Yellow)
>   - $\color{#F39C12}\text{(★ < 7.0)}$: Average (Amber / Orange)
> - **Hover Tooltips:** Episode titles are abbreviated or displayed as `...` to protect first-time viewers from spoilers. **Hover your cursor over any episode title** to view the full canonical title tooltip!

> [!WARNING]
> **Subtitles & Dub Guidance:**
> This guide is optimized for the Japanese audio + English subtitles experience. For an English dub path, consult the [One Pace Dub Watch Guide](https://redd.it/1rtpukk).

> [!NOTE]
> **Community-Curated Watchlist:**
> Following community consensus and the [One Pace Watch Guide Companion](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1zYFrLPPS3FOYrz4MDyog8R9reAsGxOqM-Sxh2Y2EvXg/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.7hgr1xkj23na), only the movies, OVAs, and specials widely regarded as **worth watching** (such as *Movie 04: Dead End Adventure*, *G-8*, *Movie 06: Baron Omatsuri*, *Monsters*, *Strong World*, *3D2Y*, *Fan Letter*, *Film Z*, *Film Gold*, *Stampede*, *Film Red*, *TV Special 15: ONE PIECE HEROINES*, and upcoming films *One Piece Film: God Valley* [2027] and *One Piece Film: BAAD* [2029]) are featured. Generic filler movies, recap retellings, and crossovers have been omitted.

> [!NOTE]
> **Fun Pace Companion Project:**
> For viewers who wish to watch filler arcs with modern pacing, **Fun Pace** edits are available via this [Fun Pace watch-order spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1LYxp1i5FldNiKdL8BKaQ3T8EeueCSpgDWvN51kCBcKI), [Mega Folder 1](https://mega.nz/folder/D2AnVboT#jDllz0HMlVOVNBLdYxEmfQ), [Mega Folder 2](https://mega.nz/folder/GM9TAICA#8r9SaFgptfSWPSDBuzSknw), [Google Drive Folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1wglxsln46TVM4LFOo4kVK910fyfl-fWS), and subtitle syncs at [fun-pace-public-subtitles](https://github.com/ang3lo-azevedo/fun-pace-public-subtitles).

> [!WARNING]
> **Netflix Live Action Spoilers:**
> Placements for the ONE PIECE Netflix Live Action series are optional companion viewings. Be aware that the live-action series introduces characters and plot revelations much earlier than the manga/anime timeline.
